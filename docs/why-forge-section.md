# Why Forge Section

The `Why Forge` section gives the homepage a short, scannable explanation of the project's value.

## Purpose

- Explain why Forge exists
- Reinforce the open-source component-library direction
- Keep the homepage easy to scan

## Structure

- Section heading
- Short supporting paragraph
- Three reason cards

## Notes

- The section is intentionally compact
- It is designed to sit below the hero
- It can be reused or expanded later if Forge grows into a larger landing page
