# Hero Section

The Forge hero section introduces the product with a clear headline, supporting copy, and two call-to-action buttons.

## Purpose

- Present Forge as an open component system
- Keep the homepage simple and direct
- Support future reuse in marketing or docs pages

## Structure

- Badge
- Headline
- Supporting text
- Primary CTA
- Secondary CTA
- Supporting preview panel

## Notes

- The section is responsive
- The content is intentionally minimal
- The component is kept reusable so it can be moved or adapted later
